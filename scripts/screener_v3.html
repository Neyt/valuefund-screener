How the grade works. Funds, shells, duplicate listings, rows with impossible values and analyses older than 30 days are removed first. Every remaining company gets a percentile score on value (45%), quality (30%) and safety (25%). Banks, insurers, REITs, biotech, software, retail, industrials and holding companies use their own factors and are compared inside their sector; the rest are compared with everyone. Grades are on a curve: A is the top 10%, B the next 20%, C the next 30%, D the next 25%, F the rest. Market caps are converted to US dollars; prices stay in the listing's currency. A grade is a reason to read the filings, not a recommendation to buy or sell. Not investment advice.
